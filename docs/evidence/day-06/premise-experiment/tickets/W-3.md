### Result
Every ticket states its premises (the files, helpers and scripts it assumes exist) in a `Premises` section, and `ticket:ready` reports a ticket without one as not ready.

### Done when
- [ ] The issue form `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/task.yml` has a required `Premises` textarea after `Why`.
- [ ] `npm run ticket:ready` reports `missing section "Premises"` for a ticket without it, like any other required section.
- [ ] Existing open tickets are not changed; the check applies to tickets labelled from now on.

### Why
Ticket #4 was written against a client script that had been superseded and a snapshot two versions old. Making the premises explicit lets a reader, or a later tool, check them before a build.

### Notes
- Ticket bodies are validated with ajv against `packages/harness/src/ticket-schema.json`, in the same way `factory.json` is validated. Add the new section to that schema and `ticket-ready.ts` picks it up.
- `factory.json` validation lives in `packages/harness/src/factory-config.ts`.
